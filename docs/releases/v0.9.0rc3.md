# DPO4000 Desk v0.9.0rc3 (release candidate)

**This is a release candidate, not the hardware-qualified stable release.** It
supersedes `v0.9.0rc2`, which has a Live View bug (below): please do not use rc2.

## What changed since rc2

- **Fixed:** Live View failed to start streaming with
  `A22 owns the instrument; Stop live streaming first`. Every Start landed in ERROR.
- Live View reads each channel at the scope's own record length (no transfer-size
  menu) and rejects a record above 1M samples before transferring it. Selecting a
  channel that is not displayed on the scope now reports which channels to turn on,
  instead of a 20-second VISA timeout.
- Faster streaming: on a DPO4054 one four-channel 100k-point group takes about
  0.72 s over TCP/IP and about 0.5 s over USB (previously about 0.97 s and 0.8 s).
- New per-channel info cards under the plot and an Auto / fixed V/div vertical grid
  (display only; the scope's vertical settings are never changed).

## Qualification status (honest summary)

- Software: offline suite and CI (Python 3.10-3.13, Windows, Ruff, PySide6) pass.
- Bench evidence on a DPO4054 (not exact-head, not independently reviewed): 1/2/4
  channel Live View at 1k/10k/100k points over TCP/IP and USB (pre-cache code) and
  TCP/IP (cached code); one 12 h four-channel TCP/IP soak passed with 0 failures.
- **Not yet done:** USB soak and post-cache USB matrix, 1M-point runs, disconnect and
  clean-close tests, the 24 h P0 soak, packaged-build screenshots, and independent
  review. See `docs/a22-test-matrix.md`.

Live View samples channels one after another: a displayed group is **not** a
simultaneous capture, and the skew between the first and last channel is shown.
